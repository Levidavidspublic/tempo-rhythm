Licensing and hosting were checked separately: a public client, example or documentation licence does not establish rights to a vendor's hosted service or model weights. UNKNOWN does not mean self-hosting is prohibited or available. No licence compatibility approval, server provisioning or production capacity measurement was performed.

To compare cost fairly, measure `total session cost / connected minutes` using the same session duration, silence ratio and text output. Include ASR, TTS, model input/output/reasoning tokens, orchestration, hosting, minimum-plan allocation and egress. Do not convert a per-character TTS price into a per-minute price without stating measured characters per minute. No numerical estimate is supplied without verified rates.

The requested GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3-Flash releases are verified in the [official Z.ai README][glm]. Their weight repository identifiers do not prove the identifiers served by Together or DeepInfra. [DeepInfra's reasoning documentation][reasoning] supports medium/high settings and states that reasoning tokens are billed as output; its examples list GLM-5.2. Exact GLM-5.3 provider availability, identifiers, request compatibility and rates remain UNKNOWN. Read model identifiers from Convex settings, do not hard-code or silently substitute another model.

Tempo's current implementation is Deepgram recognition plus sentence-by-sentence DeepInfra synthesis. Moving to Voice Agent requires protocol and audio handling changes, not only a socket URL change. The custom brain endpoint must preserve authenticated leases, conversation ownership, existing crisis interception before model calls, Accept and five-minute Undo, quotas, cancellation and privacy. Provider keys remain server-side. Test unexpected events, disconnects, stop/barge-in, stale replies and revoked sessions with fake transports before enabling. A ten-minute think-hard budget is an upper limit for research, not a promise of conversational voice latency.

Hebrew pronunciation need not be native-perfect under Amit's instruction. Clinical review is separate: the Hebrew crisis path stays OFF until Amit reviews the phrase list. ERAN and Israeli Ministry of Health guidance could not be fetched here, so this document makes no claim that a machine-drafted list is clinically validated.
