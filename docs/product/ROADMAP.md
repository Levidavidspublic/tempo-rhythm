# Roadmap

## Verdict

The product levels, in order, are Alpha, MVP, Gen 1, Gen 1.1, Gen 1.5, Gen 2, and Gen 3. Gen 1.1 and Gen 1.5 are inside Gen 1. These are not phases. A phase is a one-to-two-week project.

The current phase deadline is a usable web alpha by the end of November 2026. That date belongs to the phase. Alpha is the product level. Sources: `Downloads/nir-followup-2026-11-05/01-cursor-tempo-docs-nir-update.md`, and [../factory/FACTORY-LOOP.md](../factory/FACTORY-LOOP.md) section 7.

Dates in older roadmaps (the 2–4 Oct 2026 weekend, "next week" from 4 Oct) are history.

An exit below is UNKNOWN when Amit did not state a test. This file does not invent one.
