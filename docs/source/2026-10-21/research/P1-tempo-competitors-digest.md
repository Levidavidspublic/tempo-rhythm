# P1 digest: Tempo Flow competitors (merged, 29 Oct 2026)

Tools merged: Gemini Deep Research; Tool A (the "tempo-flow-competitive-research" file); Tool B (the "Competitive Research Brief" file); Grok DeepSearch (OCR text). I could not tell from the content which products Tool A and Tool B are.

Labels: CONFIRMED = two or more tools agree, and at least one cites a primary source. SINGLE = one tool, with a primary source. LEAD = no primary source. Never promoted. "Checked" = 29 Oct 2026 unless stated.

## Verdict

1. Brain dump, AI day planning, ADHD design and memory already exist in other apps. They are not unique.
2. Saner.ai is the closest competitor. Two of four tools pick it, and a third names it as "also close".
3. Two claims in two of the tools are false: Notion does support Hebrew with a mirrored layout, and Tiimo supports right-to-left. I checked both on the official pages.
4. The honest difference is the combination: a talking coach that also keeps your plan and notes, in English and Hebrew, built for ADHD.
5. The strongest money comparison: a US ADHD coach costs $400 to $800+ a month (ADDA, updated 5 Aug 2026). Tempo Flow costs $20 or $50.

## Analysis agreement (Q9, Q10)

All four tools agree on these points:
- Brain dump to tasks is not new. Examples: Goblin Tools, Saner.ai, Tiimo, Structured, Todoist Ramble.
- Automatic scheduling is not new. Examples: Motion, Reclaim.
- ADHD focus is not new. Examples: Tiimo, Saner.ai, Lunatask, Inflow, Goblin Tools.
- The real case is the combination in one place.
- The main objection is "why not a free list or ChatGPT?"
- The answer is: it does the data entry and keeps the plan. It is not "more intelligence".

Where they disagree:
- **Closest competitor.** Saner.ai: A and B. Tiimo: Grok, which names Saner.ai as also close. Motion: Gemini. Saner.ai has the strongest case. It is ADHD-first. It turns notes into tasks and plans the day. It allows your own AI key. Its top plan is $20, the same as Tempo Flow's basic plan.
- **Hebrew as unique.** B and Gemini call it unique. That is false, because Notion has it. A is right: Hebrew is useful, but not unique.
- **Crisis safety as unique.** B and Gemini call it unique. A notes that Inflow already links to crisis help. Safe wording: "rare among planners".
- **Voice coach as unique.** B, Gemini and Grok say yes. A notes that ChatGPT, Claude and StudyFetch already talk back. Safe wording: "no ADHD planner app in our sources has a talking coach tied to your plan".
- **Chatbots.** Gemini and Grok say chatbots cannot remember context or reach calendars. A cites OpenAI and Anthropic help pages showing that memory and connected tools exist. A is right. Do not use Gemini's line on a slide.
- **Gemini overclaims Tempo Flow features.** It says Tempo Flow monitors moods, routes to ERAN 1201, and schedules calendar events. None of that is in the brief. Drop it.
- **Bring your own ChatGPT plan.** B says users "do not pay twice". A says eligibility is UNKNOWN. Keep A's caution.

## Slide-ready content

**Who and what problem**
- Adults with ADHD know what they need to do, but struggle to keep a planner going.

**What they use today**
- Free to-do apps: Todoist (free, or $7 a month). TickTick (free, or $4.99).
- ADHD planners: Tiimo (free basic plan; Apple's iPhone App of the Year 2025). Structured ($6.99 a month).
- AI planners: Motion ($49 a month for one person). Sunsama ($22 a month).
- The closest rival, Saner.ai: $12 or $20 a month.
- Chatbots: ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro, $20 a month each.
- Human coach: US $400 to $800+ a month (ADDA, Aug 2026). Israel ₪400 a session (named coach, Oct 2026).
- All prices checked 29 Oct 2026.

**Why they'd pay**
- A coach sees you for one hour a week. Tempo Flow is there every day.
- US coaching for one month ($400 to $800) pays for 20 to 40 months of Tempo Flow at $20. EXTRAPOLATED: $400 ÷ $20 = 20; $800 ÷ $20 = 40.
- In Israel, one ₪400 session is about six months of Tempo Flow at $20. EXTRAPOLATED: $20 × 3.1 = ₪62 a month; ₪400 ÷ ₪62 = 6.5. This uses Amit's 3.1 guideline rate. Tool B used 3.7, which gives about 5 months.
- Everyday line: "Less than one coaching session buys months of a daily coach in your pocket."

**The objection, and the honest answer**
- "Why not a free list, or ChatGPT?"
- A free list still needs you to type, sort and replan. That is the hard part with ADHD.
- ChatGPT is a strong option at the same $20. It has voice and memory.
- But you must set it up and steer it every day. It is not built as an ADHD planner.
- Tempo Flow's bet: you talk, and it keeps the plan and notes for you.
- Not proven yet: will people keep using it after the 21-day trial?

**What is different (the combination)**
- A talking coach that also writes your tasks, notes and today's plan.
- English and Hebrew in one ADHD product. Of the 19 apps, only Notion has a Hebrew interface, and Notion is not ADHD-focused.
- Ready-made coach personas, from gentle to strict.
- A crisis path built in. Rare among planners; Inflow links to crisis resources.

**What is NOT different**
- Brain dump to tasks: Goblin Tools, Saner.ai, Tiimo, Structured, Todoist.
- AI scheduling: Motion, Reclaim.
- Memory across days: Notion, Akiflow, Reclaim, ChatGPT.
- AI-written pages: Notion Agent, Craft.
- Study tools: StudyFetch.
- Bring your own AI key: Saner.ai, Craft.
- Being built for ADHD: Tiimo, Saner.ai, Lunatask, Inflow.

**Closest competitor**
- Saner.ai: ADHD-first, turns notes into tasks, plans your day, $12 to $20 a month.
- Our sources show no talking coach in Saner.ai, and no Hebrew interface.

## Tool quality

- **Tool A: strongest.** It used primary sources for almost every figure, with dates and quotes. It flagged its own uncertainties, for example NotePlan's $99 vs $99.99. It never overclaimed what is unique. Every check I made agreed with A.
- **Tool B: good structure, weak sources.** Most of its prices come from review blogs, and it labels them UNVERIFIED itself. It called Hebrew UNKNOWN for all 19 apps. It overclaimed that Hebrew and crisis safety are unique. Its ADDA coach figure was correct.
- **Gemini: weakest on facts.** Its math and price columns are broken by converter errors. Wrong facts: Notion Hebrew, Tiimo RTL, the Inflow, Lunatask and Saner.ai prices, and Motion's funding. Its revenue column is invented. It adds Tempo Flow features not in the brief.
- **Grok: thin but mostly accurate on prices.** It agreed with A on Motion funding and on Notion's Hebrew. Few URLs. The OCR damaged some lines; one sentence in Q7 is unreadable. It labels most unknowns honestly.
