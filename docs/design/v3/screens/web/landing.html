What it is

Three simple parts that work together

Pages in plain Markdown

Every note, plan and list is a page you own. Tasks, habits and time blocks sit right inside the text. You can export everything as Markdown files.

Templates that do the setup for you

Start a daily page, a weekly review or a project with one choice. Ask the coach for a new template and it builds one with you.

A coach with memory that can change the app

The coach reads your pages and remembers what matters, so you don't repeat yourself. It can add a task, fill a template or build a new one. It always shows you the exact change first.

How it works

Write. Talk. Say yes.

  1. 1

    Write

    Dump what's in your head onto a page. Messy is fine. Tempo helps sort it later.

  2. 2

    Talk

    Type or speak to the coach. It knows your pages and helps you pick one next step.

  3. 3

    Accept, with Undo

    The coach suggests a change. You accept, edit or reject it. Changed your mind? Undo for 5 minutes.

Live voice

Talk it through, hands free

Start a call with the coach and just talk, in English or Hebrew. Cut in whenever you want. Every spoken reply is also written down, so you can read it back later.

Who it's for

Built for brains that work differently

For adults with ADHD, autism or learning disabilities

If starting, switching or remembering is the hard part, Tempo is for you. It paces you and gives gentle reminders in your own words.

A coach, not a therapist

Tempo helps you plan and get things done. It does not treat any condition. If things get very hard, it stops coaching and shows real places to get help.

Questions

Why do I have to ask to join?

We let people in a few at a time so we can listen and fix things fast. Ask with your email and we'll write when you're in.

Is there a password?

No. You sign in with a link we email you. Nothing to remember.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. The app, the coach and live voice work in English and Hebrew, right to left or left to right.

Can the coach change things without asking?

No. It shows you the exact change first. You accept, edit or reject it, and you can undo for 5 minutes.

What does Tempo remember about me?

Only what helps the coach help you. You can see every memory, forget any of them, and export them all as Markdown.

Is Tempo therapy?

No. Tempo is a coach for planning and doing. It is not a therapist or counselor.
