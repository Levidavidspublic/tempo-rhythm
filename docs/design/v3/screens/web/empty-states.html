Empty, loading, error and offline

Every in-between state in Tempo, in one place. Each card says which screen it belongs to.

How these states talk

  • Kind and plain. Nothing is ever her fault, and nothing counts what she missed.
  • Always one next step, as a secondary button or a link.
  • Every error says "Your data is safe." and offers Retry.
  • Offline keeps the content on screen and says when changes will save.
  • Loading shows the shape of what is coming, plus words for screen readers.
