About Tempo

A calm place for a busy brain

Tempo is an executive function aid for adults with ADHD, autism or learning disabilities. It is a coach, not a therapist or counselor.

Why it exists

Most planners are made for brains that already start, switch and remember on their own. When that is the hard part, a long list just becomes one more thing to feel bad about.

Tempo started as a tool its maker needed for his own brain. It keeps your notes and plans as simple pages, and adds a coach that remembers, paces you, and nudges you in your own words.

The coach can also change the app itself: add a task, fill a template, or build a new one. It never does this without showing you first.

What we promise

  • Calm by default

    No streaks, no badges, no red numbers. One next step at a time.

  • You decide

    The coach suggests. You accept, edit or reject. Nothing changes behind your back.

  • Everything can be undone

    Every change shows an Undo for 5 minutes.

  • Prices you can always see

    No hidden plans. We remind you before a trial ends.

  • Your words stay yours

    See everything Tempo remembers, forget any of it, and export it all as Markdown.

What Tempo is not

Tempo is not therapy and does not treat any condition. If you go three nights without sleep, or things feel like a crisis, Tempo stops coaching and shows real places to get help where you live.
